## Overview

- **Name:** Startup Village Borneo
- **Dates:** 5–9 September 2026
- **Venue:** Sheraton Kuching, Sarawak (workshops travel to Voco from Day 2)
- **Anchor partners:** Solana Foundation, SOCOE
- **Confirmed partners:** BESarawak, Solana ID / Solana Ecosystem Call,
  Superscrypt, Impossible Finance, Rarible, MonkeDAO / MonkeFoundry, Elfa AI,
  MagicBlock, TankDAO, Virtuals
- **Pending partners:** RedotPay, DWFLabs
- **Note:** TankDAO and BESarawak run no workshops.

## Daily rhythm

- Sessions start at 10:00am with a hard stop between 5:00–5:30pm.
- Evenings are free for building at the hotel.
- All talks are under 10 minutes; workshops are 30–45 minutes max.

## The Amazing Race

Points-weighted, runs all week during evenings and gaps ("your build comes first").
Teams are told upfront they may not finish everything — choose well.

**Content tasks (individual, each member post earns 10 points for the team):**
1. First impressions of Kuching — video/collage on X by 6 Sept, tag @superteamMY, @solana, SOCOE. **[10 pts]**
2. Overall impressions — video/collage on X on 10 Sept, same tags. **[no points, counts toward Content Award]**

**Amazing Race tasks (team submission via a Twitter thread tagging all members):**
3. The Bourdain pilgrimage — team photo at Choon Hui Cafe; rank a Kuching laksa vs a West Malaysian one. **[6 pts]**
4. Kek lapis at Mama Su — taste, film first bite, buy a favourite. **[4 pts]**
5. Sam's ice cream — photo eating, name the flavour of the day. **[3 pts]**
6. Ceylonese restaurant — "best cheese naan in town"; film the longest cheese pull. **[4 pts]**
7. Cats of Kuching — team photo at the cat statue. **[2 pts + 1 per additional statue]**
8. Kuching Word Sign at the Waterfront — creative photo. **[4 pts]**
9. Carpenter Street — photo with 3 unique murals. **[3 pts, 1 per mural]**
10. Kuching Waterfront — pick ≥1, max 8 pts: sampan ride filmed **[8]**, lean on flagpole **[2]**, group photo under flagpole **[3]**.
11. Darul Hana bridge — best mid-span photo. **[3 pts]**
12. Old Court House — team photo. **[3 pts]**
13. Brooke's Dockyard Heritage Museum — photo with the biggest anchor. **[3 pts]**
14. Traditional attire — photograph five Sarawakian outfits. **[5 pts, 1 per attire]**
15. Onboard a real user — teach someone a crypto product (wallet like Jupiter or RedotPay); document what was hard. Teach, don't sell. **[10 pts]**

## Ops notes

- Content prizes: final-impressions videos due 24h after the event, judged remotely.
- Sustainability track: SOCOE-aligned; criteria announced Day 2 so teams opt in early.
- Team formation: starts at Day 1 dinner, closes at Day 2 lunch; matchmaking available.
- Wallet task briefing: teach, never pressure. No approaching anyone about money/investment.
- Partners not on stage get logo placement + office-hours access.

## Implications for the product

The event structure directly shapes what the companion product must support:

- **Teams & members** with WhatsApp/social handles, formed between Day 1 dinner and Day 2 lunch.
- **A weighted, evolving leaderboard** for the Amazing Race with per-task scoring rules,
  bonuses, and per-station caps.
- **Submission intake** tied to X/Twitter threads, with a hard cutoff at 18:00 on Day 4.
- **Schedule surfacing** with the fixed daily rhythm and per-day agenda.
- **Judging & demo-day** support for Day 5.
- **Solana wallet onboarding** aligned with task #15 and the Solana-first ecosystem.
